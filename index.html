04 / ABOUT

Design that starts with the player.

I’m Darryl Lewis, a game designer and technical designer interested in the space where clear rules create surprising play.

My portfolio centers on reusable combat and elemental systems, and independent scenarios that explore what players can do with them. Additional work spans AI, UX, and implementation. I’m especially interested in how mechanics connect, how players understand them, and how iteration makes those connections stronger.
